Public launches

Ten public access points around the bay. Which one you should use today is a wind question, and the launches page works through it: put in on the shore the wind is coming from, because that shore has no fetch behind it.

LaunchZonePosition
Lake Huron DNR Launch, Patterson Road
Bay City. At the mouth of the Saginaw River. Paved ramps with three docks, busy on weekends, no wake zone up and down the river. The most flexible starting point because the river is right there when the bay is unfishable.

Linwood Beach Marina
Linwood. Well kept and efficient even on holiday weekends. The classic west side jumping off point into the middle of the inner bay.
Inner Bay43.7332, -83.9466
Bay County Pinconning Park
Pinconning. County park with a launch, beach, and boardwalk. Day use fee. Good option when you want the north end of the inner bay.
Inner Bay43.8532, -83.9244
Pine River Access
Standish. Small boat ramp near the top of the inner bay. Narrow road, limited parking, five to seven feet of water off the ramp.
Lower Bay43.9766, -83.8564
Au Gres Public Access, Point Lookout Harbor
Au Gres. Harbor of refuge with well maintained ramps and plenty of parking. Access east to open Lake Huron or west up the river. The main gateway to the lower bay.
Lower Bay44.0270, -83.6793
Quanicassee area ramp, Barney Drive
Fairgrove. Two ramps with docks on the shallow south end of the east side. Quiet, and the closest east shore access to Bay City.
Eastern Bay43.5847, -83.6809
Fish Point Wildlife Refuge
Unionville. State wildlife area with marsh, an observation tower, and ditch fishing. The bay itself is not reachable by car here, so treat it as shore access and birding rather than a launch.
Eastern Bay43.6966, -83.5266
Sebewaing Harbor Marina
Sebewaing. Two docks, four unloading spots, bait shop on site. The traditional east side launch and the one that clears quickly even when busy.
Eastern Bay43.7344, -83.4586
Bay Port Public Boating Access
Bay Port. Launch plus extensive shore fishing off the docks. Long standing perch reputation, and a fish company in town.
Eastern Bay43.8542, -83.3743
Caseville Harbor
Caseville. Full harbor at the top of the east shore near the transition to the outer bay. Everything within walking distance.
Eastern Bay43.9428, -83.2749

Named water

In a bay with very little structure, people navigate by names. Some are real features, some are road ends projected out onto open water. Either way these are the words used at the ramp and in the state fishing reports.

PlaceZoneApproximate position
Callahan Reef
Hard structure in open water, at the boundary between the lower and eastern bay. Fish relate to it and so does everybody else.
Lower Bay43.8600, -83.6200
Spoils Island
Dredge spoil island off the south east shore. Named repeatedly in state fishing reports for walleye in about ten feet.
Eastern Bay43.6800, -83.7800
Wildfowl Bay
Shallow protected water behind the islands on the east side. Perch, pike, and extensive reeds and bulrush.
Eastern Bay43.7900, -83.4200
Shipping channel, outer reach
The dredged channel running out from the river mouth. The one piece of real structure in the inner bay, and a working channel with commercial traffic.
Inner Bay43.7200, -83.7900
Dredge Island, river mouth
Rip rap edges near the mouth that hold baitfish. First structure post spawn fish meet on the way back out.
Inner Bay43.6500, -83.8500

The lower bay is also described by road ends carried straight out from shore, Thomas Road, Finn Road, and Vasser Road among them. Those are bearings people run rather than fixed points, so they are named on the lower bay page rather than pinned here.
